Add To An Existing Case

Send us further documents or information on a case you have already opened with us.

Preview

This form has not been built yet. The page is here so the layout can be seen alongside the new-case form. For now, please email your additional documents to your RIAC, or call us. Back to the intake choices.

Step 1

What to send

Anything that has changed or arrived since you first wrote to us. Most often that is:

  • A fingerprint RAP sheet that was not available when you opened the case.
  • Further accusatory instruments, superseding charges, or new police reports.
  • Immigration documents your client has since produced.
  • A revised offer or likely disposition — particularly if the charge weight or type has changed.
  • An answer to something we asked you when we replied.
Step 2

Tell us which case

So we can attach what you send to the right file, the form will ask for your client’s name and date of birth as you originally gave them to us.
