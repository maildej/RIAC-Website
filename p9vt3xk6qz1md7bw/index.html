Intake Forms

Tell us about a case you would like advice on. Submitting online is the quickest way to reach us — or download a fillable form to complete offline and send in.

Preview

This is a preview page while the online intake is being built. The layout is still being worked on. The form behind it is live, so anything sent from here reaches the Central New York RIAC exactly as a normal intake would. The published intake page is nyriac.com/intake.html.

What would you like to do?

Choose whichever applies. One form covers every kind of case, criminal and non-criminal alike. If you are unsure which of the two below you need, start a new case — we will match it up at our end.

Start a new case

Ask us to advise on a case we have not seen before — criminal or non-criminal, one form covers both. Allow about fifteen minutes, and have your client’s immigration documents to hand.

Begin

Add to an existing case

Send us further documents or information on a case you have already opened with us — a new RAP sheet, a revised offer, or an answer to something we asked. You will need your RIAC case number.

Begin
These forms go to the Central New York RIAC (Region 2). If your case is outside our sixteen counties, please find your RIAC and contact them directly — the other centers are not yet taking intakes through this website.

If you cannot submit online

Some of our colleagues complete intakes at a jail with no signal, or prefer to write them out by hand. Both forms below are fillable PDFs that work offline — download the one you need before you set off if you are going somewhere without a connection.

Criminal Cases

RIAC intake form to be used in all criminal matters (from a parking ticket to A-I Felony).

Non-Criminal Cases

RIAC intake form for all non-criminal cases, such as family court, divorce matters, affirmative immigration applications etc.
